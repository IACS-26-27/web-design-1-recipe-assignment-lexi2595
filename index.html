Recipe For A Nonprofit

-A gift from lexi2595-

INGREDIENTS

  • A charitable cause
  • An engaged community
  • A business plan
  • A board
  • A nonprofit name
  • Initial funding
  • Official paperwork
  • Bylaws
  • Conflict of interest policy

STEP 1: Clarify your cause

Be specific with your cause.

Who do you work for? Children in poverty? Women with breast cancer? Animals?

What do you do for them? House them? Make handmade gifts?

How do you do that? Do volunteers help you? Do you accept donations? How do you deliver products?

Further develop your WHAT and HOW in the next step.

STEP 2: Understand your community

What are the primary needs of your target group? What are their wants?
