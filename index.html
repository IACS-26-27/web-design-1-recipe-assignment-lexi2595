Recipe For A Nonprofit

-A gift from lexi2595-

INGREDIENTS

  • A charitable cause
  • A community
  • A business plan
  • A board
  • A nonprofit name
  • Initial funding
  • Official paperwork
  • Bylaws
  • Conflict of interest policy

STEP 1: Clarify your goal

Be specific with your goal.

Who will you work for? Children in poverty? Women with breast cancer? Animals?

Do some further brainstorming.

What could you do for them? House them? Make handmade gifts?

How could you do that? Would volunteers help you? Would you accept donations? How would you deliver products?

What is your ultimate goal by creating this nonprofit?

STEP 2: Understand your community

What are the primary needs of your target group? What are their wants?

Do some research. Are they struggling with a certain security? Do they wish they had a luxury that isn't currently accessible to them?

Once you know what they need and want, move on to the next step.

STEP 3: Raise awareness

Before starting a business, you need to guarentee interest. Nonprofits are similar.

If people don't know about the suffering cancer causes, or how many dogs and cats are put down due to overcrowding, who will support your nonprofit?

STEP 4: Make a plan

What can you actually offer your target group? Awareness? Housing? Supplies? Events?

What skills do you have to help them? What tasks will you need help with?

For example, if you aim to help animals but you can't afford a physical building, an online or at-home model is probably best.

Think about the financial aspects too. How will you raise money? How much will various categories cost? How will you afford things when donations are slow?

STEP 5: Form your board of directors

Decide the ideal board size to get things done. Form that group.

  • Don't solely choose your friends as members.
  • Have a diverse cast to prevent socially irresponsible decisions.
  • Choose members who are genuinely passionate about the cause.
  • Have a variety of people with different skills on the board.

You will need a president, a treasurer, and a secretary.

Make sure everyone is on board (excuse the pun) before continuing.

STEP 6: Decide on a name

A nonprofit with a name is good, but a nonprofit with a memorable name is better.

Choose a short, catchy name that connects back to your cause.

STEP 7: Gather initial funding

In step 4, you should have already decided how you will fund your nonprofit.

You also need to figure out where inital capital, for things like upfront costs and filing fees, will come from.

Will you provide it yourself? From a consistent job or side gigs?

Make your estimations and raise the needed funds.

STEP 8: Write your bylaws and conflict of interest policy

Write your bylaws, the laws your nonprofit will always follow, and your conflict of interest policy.

(This article from Charity Lawyer has some tips for writing your bylaws!)

STEP 9: Complete paperwork and filing

File for nonprofit 501(c)(3) status in your state and on the federal level.

Find the correct paperwork on the IRS government website.

STEP 10: Maintain your nonprofit

Nonprofit organizations can't survive without ongoing work and support.

Keep your board involved, follow your bylaws, and work towards your goal.
